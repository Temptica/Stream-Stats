Commands and redeems

Everything you can trigger on stream: chat commands, channel point redeems and clicking the overlay. If a cost or cooldown on Twitch differs from this page, Twitch is right.

Chat commands

CommandWhat happensHow to useCooldown
!commands!help, !command The bot lists the available commands. none
!bm!ballamachina, !wishlist Info about Balla Machina, the pinball roguelike deck-builder Temptica is making, with the Steam wishlist link. 10 s
!credits!credit, !assets Who made the art and tools used on the overlay. 30 s
!tts Your message is read out loud by the text-to-speech voice, in the alert queue. !tts <message>. Pick voices inline: {alba} {jean} {george} {mary} {eve} {michael}. Mix them: {alba} psst {george} WHAT? none
!points!point, !punten How many points you have. You earn points by clicking fish on the overlay. 10 s each
!otter!tama, !tamagotter The Tamagotter's needs (hunger, thirst, energy, happiness) and what it needs most, or that it is doing great. 10 s
!setso Sets the personal text shown when someone gives you a shoutout. !setso [primary/blue/green/orange/purple] your text sets it (color optional, max 500 characters). !setso shows yours, !setso clear removes it. 10 s each

"each" means the cooldown is per viewer; otherwise it is shared by the whole chat.

Clicking the overlay

With the Heat extension you can click right on the stream:

  • Click a fish to catch it and earn points: normal fish 1, gold fish 2, rainbow fish 5. Check them with !points.
  • Click a bubble to pop it. Clicking empty space sometimes spawns a new one.
  • Boop the otter's nose or pat its head.
  • In October, caught fish and popped candy bubbles also give candy. Check it with !candy.
